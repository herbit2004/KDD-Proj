# COMP5331 KDD Project Options

Six candidate projects for the COMP5331 group project: three paper implementations and three research proposals. Each directory contains an English proposal, a Chinese feasibility brief, and a sourced reading list. The proposals describe separate alternatives; the group will select one.

| Option | Type and area | Main paper and further reading | Planned work | Code, data, compute | Main consideration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [I1: OVO/OVR](implementation-ovo-ovr/) | Implementation · Topic 2: Classification | Chen & Lin, ICDM 2025 | Compare one-versus-one and one-versus-rest kernel classifiers under controlled class imbalance; report macro-F1, balanced accuracy and per-class recall. | Author code ZIP; LIBSVM/UCI/KEEL data; CPU. | Match preprocessing, tuning and imbalance protocols. |
| [I2: ExDBSCAN](implementation-exdbscan/) | Implementation · Topic 1: Clustering | Matthews et al., KDD 2026 | Reproduce counterfactual validity, proximity and diversity on synthetic and small public tabular data. | Public author repository; OpenML/UCI data; CPU. | Repository has no stated license and benchmark inputs need reconstruction. |
| [I3: TABFAIRGDT](implementation-tabfairgdt/) | Implementation · Topic 7: Fairness, Equity and Justice | Panagiotou et al., ICDM 2025 | Run the decision-tree generator and selected baselines; measure speed, utility and demographic fairness. | MIT author repository; public tabular data; CPU. | Fairness results depend strongly on dataset and downstream classifier. |
| [R1: Graph anomalies](research-graph-anomalies/) | Research · Topic 1: Clustering | Latapy & Rajeh, ICDM 2025; Poštuvan et al., TMLR 2024 | Test whether simple graph features remain effective for structured, rather than random, anomalies; study low-cost feature selection. | GPL-2.0 author repository; generated graphs and public data; CPU. | Define realistic injections without leaking labels into features. |
| [R2: Density-aware RAE](research-density-aware-rae/) | Research · Topic 4: Vector Search | Zhang & Zhao, RAE preprint; McInnes et al., UMAP | Measure neighborhood preservation by density and evaluate density-aware sampling against unchanged RAE. | MIT repository; public vectors; CPU or small GPU. | Training runtime and paper-version discrepancy require early checks. |
| [R3: Fair communities](research-fair-community/) | Research · Topic 7: Fairness, Equity and Justice | Gkartzios et al., ICDM 2025; Gkartzios et al., WWW 2025; MOUFLON, 2026 | Examine modularity–fairness trade-offs on unequal-group graphs and test adaptive fairness weighting. | Public author repository with sample data; synthetic and public graphs; small GPU helpful. | Strong recent related work makes the novelty boundary narrow. |

For a group seeking a reliable implementation, **I1** is the simplest starting point, followed by **I3** if the group wants a richer fairness evaluation. **I2** is attractive for clustering but needs more engineering around data and dependencies. Among the research options, **R1** offers the clearest controlled experiment and lowest compute burden; **R3** has strong thematic alignment with graph/KG interests but needs careful positioning against recent fair-community work; **R2** is the highest training-risk option. These are comparative judgments, not guaranteed outcomes.

The proposal format follows the [2026 Canvas Project Proposal](https://canvas.ust.hk/courses/72132/pages/project-proposal) and the [course Overview](https://canvas.ust.hk/courses/72132/): title, project type, group and member information, distinction from members' own research/FYP where applicable, a 1,000–2,000-word description, and a reading list. Member fields remain to be completed before submission. The [Suggested Topics](https://canvas.ust.hk/courses/72132/pages/project-suggestedtopics) page asks groups to define a specific title under a selected topic; papers chosen independently for the project require instructor approval. Each option here begins with a paper on that list, while related literature supports the focused question.

Each `references/README.md` identifies source URLs and the status of each PDF. The repository includes only papers with an explicitly checked redistribution basis; other full texts are obtained from the cited publisher or author links.
