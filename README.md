# COMP5331 KDD Project Options

Six candidate projects for the COMP5331 group project: three paper implementations and three research proposals. Each directory contains an English proposal, a Chinese feasibility brief, and a sourced reading list. The proposals describe separate alternatives; the group will select one.

| Option | Type and area | Main paper and further reading | Planned work | Code, data, compute | Main consideration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [I1: OVO/OVR](implementation-ovo-ovr/) | Implementation · Topic 2: Classification | Chen & Lin, ICDM 2025; Hsu & Lin; Rifkin & Klautau | Compare one-versus-one and one-versus-rest kernel classifiers under controlled class imbalance; report macro-F1, balanced accuracy and per-class recall. | Author ZIP contains environment, download, training and result scripts; raw data fetched separately; CPU. | **Low–medium:** full pipeline is documented, but the published run has not been verified locally. |
| [I2: ExDBSCAN](implementation-exdbscan/) | Implementation · Topic 1: Clustering | Matthews et al., KDD 2026; DBSCAN; BayCon; additional material | Reproduce fixed-clustering counterfactual validity, proximity and graph-aware diversity on small data. | Core method is public; benchmark driver expects missing files; OpenML data; CPU. | **Medium–high:** no code license or complete runnable benchmark; requires a new experiment driver. |
| [I3: TABFAIRGDT](implementation-tabfairgdt/) | Implementation · Topic 7: Fairness, Equity and Justice | Panagiotou et al., ICDM 2025; FairGAN; imbalance study; Abroshan et al. | Run the decision-tree generator and selected baselines; measure speed, utility and demographic fairness. | MIT repo has example, experiment and data-fetching code; CPU; full baseline environment is heavy. | **Medium:** evaluation chain and dependencies are more involved than I1. |
| [R1: Graph anomalies](research-graph-anomalies/) | Research · Topic 1: Clustering | Latapy & Rajeh, ICDM 2025; Poštuvan et al.; MIDAS | Test simple graph features against controlled structured anomalies and cost-aware feature selection. | GPL-2.0 four-stage pipeline and examples; generated or public link streams; CPU. | **Medium:** injections must avoid timing, degree and future-information shortcuts. |
| [R2: Density-aware RAE](research-density-aware-rae/) | Research · Topic 4: Vector Search | Zhang & Zhao, KDD 2026 (available PDF has an older ICLR header); UMAP; self-tuning spectral clustering; QPAD | Measure neighborhood preservation by density and evaluate density-aware sampling against unchanged RAE. | MIT training/baseline code, but loaders require specific precomputed embeddings; small GPU useful. | **High:** public small vectors need a new adapter; original benchmark embeddings are not included. |
| [R3: Fair communities](research-fair-community/) | Research · Topic 7: Fairness, Equity and Justice | Gkartzios et al., ICDM 2025; DMoN; group modularity; MOUFLON | Examine modularity–fairness trade-offs for unequal **binary** groups and test adaptive weight selection. | Public code and toy data, no stated license; synthetic graphs and a documented real graph; small GPU useful. | **Medium–high:** code assumes 0/1 groups, and recent work narrows the novelty claim. |

The selected readings are:

- **I1:** Chen and Lin, [*Revisiting One-Versus-One and One-Versus-Rest*](implementation-ovo-ovr/references/README.md); Hsu and Lin, *A Comparison of Methods for Multi-class Support Vector Machines*; Rifkin and Klautau, *In Defense of One-Vs-All Classification*.
- **I2:** Matthews et al., [*ExDBSCAN: Explaining DBSCAN with Counterfactual Reasoning*](implementation-exdbscan/references/README.md) and its additional material; Ester et al., the original DBSCAN paper; Romashov et al., *BayCon*.
- **I3:** Panagiotou et al., [*TABFAIRGDT*](implementation-tabfairgdt/references/README.md); Xu et al., *FairGAN*; Panagiotou et al., the tabular-data imbalance study; Abroshan et al., *Imposing Fairness Constraints in Synthetic Data Generation*.
- **R1:** Latapy and Rajeh, [*Trivial Graph Features and Classical Learning are Enough to Detect Random Anomalies*](research-graph-anomalies/references/README.md); Poštuvan et al., *Learning-Based Link Anomaly Detection in Continuous-Time Dynamic Graphs*; Bhatia et al., *MIDAS*.
- **R2:** Zhang and Zhao, [*RAE: A Neural Network Dimensionality Reduction Method for Nearest Neighbors Preservation in Vector Search*](research-density-aware-rae/references/README.md); McInnes et al., *UMAP*; Zelnik-Manor and Perona, *Self-Tuning Spectral Clustering*; Fu and Zhao, *QPAD*.
- **R3:** Gkartzios et al., [*Modularity-Fair Deep Community Detection*](research-fair-community/references/README.md); Tsitsulin et al., *Graph Clustering with Graph Neural Networks*; Gkartzios et al., *Fair Network Communities through Group Modularity*; Panayiotou et al., *MOUFLON*.

For a group seeking a reliable implementation, **I1** is the simplest starting point, followed by **I3** if the group wants a richer fairness evaluation. **I2 is not a low-effort reproduction** in the currently released repository. Among the research options, **R1** offers the clearest controlled experiment and lowest compute burden; **R3** fits graph/KG interests but is restricted to two groups with the present code; **R2** has the largest data-interface and training risk. These are source-code and documentation assessments, not completed end-to-end reproductions.

The proposal format follows the [2026 Canvas Project Proposal](https://canvas.ust.hk/courses/72132/pages/project-proposal) and the [course Overview](https://canvas.ust.hk/courses/72132/): title, project type, group and member information, distinction from members' own research/FYP where applicable, a 1,000–2,000-word description, and a reading list. Member fields remain to be completed before submission. The [Suggested Topics](https://canvas.ust.hk/courses/72132/pages/project-suggestedtopics) page asks groups to define a specific title under a selected topic; papers chosen independently for the project require instructor approval. Each option here begins with a paper on that list, while related literature supports the focused question.

Each `references/README.md` identifies source URLs and the status of each PDF. The repository includes only papers with an explicitly checked redistribution basis; other full texts are obtained from the cited publisher or author links. The ExDBSCAN KDD main paper is readable in Canvas and separately identified from its arXiv additional material; its PDF has not yet been saved in the local paper collection.
